# Code of Conduct

WP Translation Checker (WTC) adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/) as the baseline for participation in this project.

Contributor Covenant 3.0 is stewarded by the Organization for Ethical Source and is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in WTC project spaces, including GitHub Issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions.

When collaborating through Making WordPress community spaces, participants must also follow the rules and expectations that apply to those spaces.

## Expected collaboration

Contributors, reviewers, translators, and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, engage honestly, respect different viewpoints and experience levels, give and receive constructive feedback, and keep discussion focused on the project and its users.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualized conduct, disclosure of another person's private information, threats, or other conduct that makes collaboration unsafe is not acceptable.

## Reporting

To report a possible Code of Conduct violation involving WTC project spaces, contact the WTC maintainer privately by direct message to **@Yamabiko** in the [Making WordPress Slack](https://make.wordpress.org/chat/).

Do not use a public Issue to report conduct concerns that require confidentiality.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review reports privately and may take proportionate action such as a warning, temporary participation restriction, removal of content, or a ban from project-managed spaces.

The project will prioritize participant safety and confidentiality when handling reports.
